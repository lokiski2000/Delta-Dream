Delta Dream

Delta Dream is an app designed (by AI) to make practicing CANWILD (Custom Alarm Noise Wake-Induced Lucid Dreaming) techniques much easier.

By automating timed, customizable audio cues while you sleep, Delta Dream helps you trigger lucidity without needing to wake up and manually turn off an alarm.

🚀 How to Use

Download and install the app.

Open Delta Dream and configure your preferred settings.

Save your settings.

Upload the audio cues you would like to use.

Tap Start Time Interval before going to sleep.

⚙️ Recommended Settings

For the best CANWILD experience, try starting with this configuration:

Initial Delay Interval: ON — Set to random between 30 to 40 minutes (gives you enough time to fall asleep first).

Interval Loops: Set to Infinite.

Time Interval: Set to 10 minutes (plays your cue every 10 minutes after the initial delay).

Audio Cues: Upload the custom audio cues you find most effective.

💡 Pro-Tip: Calibrating Your Volume

Before going to bed, it is crucial to find the "sweet spot" for your audio cue volume—loud enough for your sleeping mind to notice and integrate into a dream, yet quiet enough that it won't fully wake you up.

Lie down in your normal sleeping position.

Tap the Test Audio button located at the top right of the app.

Adjust your device volume until the cue is subtle, clear, and non-startling.
